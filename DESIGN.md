# TIMELESS visual direction

## Character

Editorial, nocturnal, and quietly luxurious. Let typography, strong photography, and generous scale carry the identity rather than decorative frames.

## Palette and type

- Background: near-black charcoal (`#11110f`).
- Secondary surface: deep charcoal (`#20201c`).
- Primary text: warm ivory (`#f2eee6`).
- Accent: muted gold (`#c3a477` / `#ddc49c`), reserved for primary actions and limited emphasis.
- Implement these color tokens in OKLCH, retaining hex fallbacks for older browsers.
- Display type: Playfair Display.
- Supporting type: DM Sans.

## Layout

- Keep shared, even outer margins and align content to a consistent page frame.
- Use a consistent 4px spacing rhythm for layout and component spacing.
- Keep hero imagery separate from copy: a full-height photograph beside text on desktop, and photo-first on narrow screens.
- Use asymmetry within the grid for editorial character, not to create an empty half-screen.
- Use distinct compositions for the introduction, experience list, visit details, and photo-led invitation.
- Keep experience highlights as open typography-led content, not nested or outlined cards.
- Avoid eyebrow labels, tiny tracked caps, index numbers, hairline dividers, decorative gradients, and italic accent words in headings.
- Keep body text readable and maintain clear keyboard focus.

## Motion

Use restrained opacity and transform motion only. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing animation and transitions while keeping all content visible.

## Calls to action

Make WhatsApp reservation the primary action. Keep secondary navigation plain and descriptive.
